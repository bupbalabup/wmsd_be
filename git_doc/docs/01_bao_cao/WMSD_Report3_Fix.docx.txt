Definition and Acronyms
WMSD = Warehouse Management System Development; SRS = Software Requirements Specification; UC = Use Case; FE = Feature; BF = Business Flow; SO = copied external Sales Order; POD = historical standalone Proof of Delivery, outside the selected workflow; PAY-CFM = external payment-receipt confirmation; RR = Replenishment Request; PO = Purchase Order; GR = Goods Receipt; WIR = historical Warehouse Issue Request; OD = historical Outbound Delivery; PGI = Post Goods Issue; TR = Transfer Request; TD = Transfer Dispatch; MWTR = destination transfer receipt; ADJ = actual nonzero stocktake quantity adjustment; FD = historical standalone failed-delivery case; GR-FD = runtime kind for actual unreceived-goods receipt on an issue; RECLASS = total-preserving quality/location reclassification at B1 closure; UOM = unit catalogue entry (code, name, carton flag, optional area mm²); Conversion = versioned base-unit factor per product and optional lot; Document ID = [document type]-[warehouse or department code]-[YYMMDD]-[sequence], stored as the document _id.
Warehouse Staff is the canonical existing warehouse application role. Put-away/picking tasks may retain TO as a historical task abbreviation; no redundant inter-warehouse TO layer. Custody is physically held supplier-owned stock or historical customer-owned stock; new CR inspection has no custody balance. Transit is a stock-account state, not a quality grade. Available stock additionally meets verified identity, GOOD quality, eligible location, ownership, freeze and reservation conditions. N–X–T means Inbound–Outbound–Closing quantity report.

I. Overall Requirements
1. Context Diagram
Sales Orders and customer returns remain in scope. Customer catalogue management and the separate delivery workflow are excluded. Buyer/contact information belongs to the individual source order, goods issue or return; new documents must not require creating or selecting a customer master record. The sales chain is SO -> direct Goods Issue. Receipt evidence and actual unreceived-goods intake belong to that issue, without separate WIR, outbound delivery, sales picking or failed-delivery worklists. Historical records remain traceable.

This is the user's scope decision of 17 November 2026. It defines requirements; this documentation change does not remove existing code, collections, permissions or stored references. The current runtime contracts describe compatibility until implementation is aligned.

Five default roles, plus custom roles that the Administrator builds only from the fixed permission catalogue, with exactly one role assigned to each user: Sales copies verified external SO, handles customer returns, records issue-linked customer receipt evidence (UC-105) and confirms external payment receipt (UC-176); assigned Warehouse records direct goods issues and physical receipts; Accountant agrees PO terms with the supplier outside WMSD, then records, issues and reschedules the PO and closes the unreceived remainder, and records supplier-return agreements/ACK and external accounting references; Owner makes scoped decisions, clears differences and maintains products, UOM/conversions and the warehouse/location catalogue; Administrator manages accounts (lock with reason), custom roles and permissions without business-posting rights.
MISA, Customer and Supplier are staff-mediated business parties/systems, not direct API/login actors. Commercial pricing policy, invoices/refunds, payment execution, debt and accounting remain external; WMSD keeps the copied SO unit selling price only for permitted roles. WMSD SO is a manually copied source record; Issue-linked receipt evidence and PAY-CFM record fulfilment without executing payment or managing delivery trips. No automatic channel/MISA integration. D2/D3 retain personal departmental entry and five roles despite survey-reported shared entry/delegation.
API-01 SMTP sends post-commit warehouse notifications. API-02 controlled storage supports private evidence upload/retrieval; the implementation stores authenticated Cloudinary assets and issues short-lived signed download URLs only after document/warehouse scope checks.
Password recovery by email has two interactions. Staff can request a one-time password (OTP) from Sign In (UC-152, implemented), and the Administrator can request an OTP for a selected account (UC-175, planned). In both flows, WMSD sends the OTP to the account's registered email address and the account holder uses it to choose a new password.

2. Main Business Flow
The following eight business flows define actors, source documents, alternate outcomes and inventory effects. Operational demand, physical execution, approval and external financial confirmation remain distinct.

2.1 BF-01 — Replenishment, purchasing and goods receipt

Trigger: a saved SO has uncovered quantity after checking compatible timely sources and existing requests; or availability remains below its warehouse minimum after committed stocktake closure/unlock; or Sales/Warehouse records another reasoned need.

Sales Staff or Warehouse Staff may also open Create replenishment request directly from the menu for a reasoned other need, without a sales shortage, a low-stock alert or a stocktake result. An RR may consist entirely of MANUAL_OTHER lines. For each such line, select a product and enter a positive quantity and unit. The request requires a receiving warehouse, a need date and a reason at the request level. Line reasonNote is optional (up to 2,000 characters). Whenever the server evaluation raises a warning (OPEN_REQUEST, INCOMING_PO or STOCK_SUFFICIENT), the creator must confirm the independent need and enter an explanation before Save draft/Submit. Review current stock, existing requests and incoming supply to avoid duplicate purchasing. Availability at or above the minimum does not by itself block this path. Do not invent SO/stocktake references or source-specific evaluation snapshots for MANUAL_OTHER. Submit to a different Store Owner for approval; Accountant creates the PO only after approval. RR creation and approval do not change stock.

**Main flow:**

1. For each line the system evaluates, for the same receiving warehouse and product: available GOOD/STORE stock in pickable STORAGE (net of reservations, open tasks, defect holds and frozen locations), the warehouse minimum/target, open RR quantity (DRAFT, SUBMITTED or approved but not yet ordered), outstanding OPEN/PARTIAL PO quantity with its common expected date, and other-warehouse availability within the user's scope. The result is advisory: OPEN_REQUEST, INCOMING_PO and STOCK_SUFFICIENT warnings never block on their own. Line sources are MANUAL_OTHER (default), STOCK_THRESHOLD, SALES_SHORTAGE (saved SO or WIR line) or STOCKTAKE_LOW_STOCK (closed stocktake); SO and stocktake references are kept for traceability. A SO source does not require WIR first.
2. Create request opens one form. One RR has one receiving warehouse and need date. Units come from the active UOM catalogue and the current product/lot conversion; a carton unit requires a verified lot. The creator decides the requested quantity. Opening/cancelling persists no RR. On Save draft/Submit the server re-evaluates every line inside the transaction; a warned line without independentNeedConfirmed and a reason is rejected while entered fields are preserved for review. A saved source-backed line cannot change its source document; remove it and add a new line instead.
3. Save draft records DRAFT. Submit records the sender, version and per-line warning snapshot as SUBMITTED. An independent Store Owner approves line quantities within the requested amount or rejects the submitted request with a reason. Rejection is final. The creator may withdraw a DRAFT or SUBMITTED request, and the Store Owner may cancel an APPROVED request with no linked PO. Approval permits PO creation and leaves stock unchanged.
4. The Accountant agrees quantity, price and delivery date with the supplier outside WMSD, then creates the PO from the approved RR. A request can have one linked PO at a time for one supplier and the receiving warehouse. Each RR line appears at most once. The smallest whole-carton quantity covering the approved amount may exceed it. The Accountant can edit the PO in DRAFT, issue it as OPEN, and reschedule its common expected date in OPEN or PARTIAL with a reason. Cancelling an eligible PO releases the RR for another PO.
5. For every delivery, Warehouse records one goods arrival with its inspection: arrival time, deliverer and delivery reference; per PO line the actual verified lot (a PO line delivered in several lots is split here), the counted quantity in carton or piece units converted with the lot conversion, quality, packaging SEALED/OPENED/TORN/UNKNOWN and ownership decision ACCEPT/CUSTODY/REJECT. Allowance is checked in base units. A PO may have multiple arrivals. Q8 permits Owner/Accountant co-inspection recorded in evidence; GR posting stays with Warehouse. Torn packaging alone is not a tile defect; opened cartons require actual piece counts.
6. Split counted quantities by lot, quality and ownership decision. Only GOOD goods can be accepted into store ownership. DAMAGED, PENDING and BLOCKED goods must use CUSTODY. Record evidence for shortages, over-deliveries and discrepancies.
7. Confirming inspection creates a draft purchase GR for GOOD / ACCEPT lines and a draft supplier custody receipt for CUSTODY lines. Confirmation leaves stock unchanged. Posting the purchase GR adds store-owned stock at an eligible RECEIVING location. Posting custody records supplier-owned stock at CUSTODY or QUARANTINE and leaves the PO received quantity unchanged. Both postings check location capacity and stocktake locks. Warehouse Staff can initiate a supplier return from a posted custody line under BF-08.
8. Warehouse Staff opens BF-03 relocation for a posted purchase receipt and moves stock from RECEIVING to STORAGE in the same warehouse. Confirming the actual movement updates both locations and preserves the total stock, lot, quality and owner.
9. After a purchase receipt is posted, the PO is PARTIAL while quantity remains outstanding. When all quantities are settled, it is CLOSED if any quantity was short-closed and COMPLETED otherwise. The Accountant can close unreceived quantities in one command with a reason and optional evidence. Warehouse Staff can reverse an eligible posted receipt. A CLOSED PO stays CLOSED after reversal. The Accountant can reopen it if an outstanding quantity remains, while previously short-closed quantities stay closed.

Exceptions: shortage leaves remainder open; over-delivery stays separate pending acceptance; a different manufacturer lot is not substituted merely because SKU names match; zero accepted quantity still has a discrepancy case; return of previously received goods uses BF-08; replacements are new linked receipts.

Inventory effect: RR, approval, PO and inspection confirmation leave stock unchanged. Posting records stock once under the correct owner. BF-03 relocation changes the stock location and preserves the total.

Documents: RR, PO, arrival/inspection, purchase GR, supplier custody receipt, relocation order and discrepancy evidence. Reference: SAP WM I pp. 10–11, 18–22 and [MISA purchase receipt](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-su-dung-tinh-nang-nhap-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop). Lot identity (product + manufacturer lot with fixed pieces per carton) and permissions are WMSD-specific controls.

2.2 BF-02 — Defect detection, isolation and disposition

**Trigger:** cracks/quality defects discovered in BF-01/03/04/05/06/07 or routine work.

1. Warehouse records source, lot, location, quantity, symptoms, time and photos/record.
2. Immediately record allocation blocking and a quarantine case without awaiting Owner approval. In B1 frozen stocktake scope, initially record defect hold/evidence only; quality/location changes use the approved BF-05 closure batch, never ordinary posting to bypass the freeze. Retain blocking until reclassification/quarantine completes. Replan affected reservations and notify Sales.
3. Verify condition and ownership/custody. Quarantine alone never transfers ownership.
4. Warehouse proposes release after reinspection, regrade, supplier return or destruction. Supplier return follows BF-08 with Accountant-recorded agreement, without Owner dispatch approval; destruction/regrade/release outside stocktake still uses step 5 Owner authorization. Discounted sales remain external and do not turn defects into good stock.
5. For destruction/regrade/release outside stocktake, Owner approves precise action/quantity or requests evidence; otherwise retain isolation. Supplier return bypasses this approval. Inside a locked stocktake scope, differences are settled only through stocktake difference approval (UC-139) rather than duplicate UC-111 approval.
6. Warehouse confirms authorized destruction/regrade/release; destruction requires actual evidence/quantity. Supplier return goes to BF-08 on Accountant/supplier agreement without Owner approval. In B1 quality/location posts only through stocktake closure, never ordinary UC-112.
7. Post exactly once. Close only when every quantity is resolved or linked to an accountable open case.

Inventory effect: quarantine/regrade preserves store-owned totals. Destruction reduces actual destroyed quantity; supplier return deducts in BF-08, not again here. Previously deducted quantities cannot be deducted through another case.

Documents: defect case, inspection, disposition decision, destruction record/return issue. These controls address tiles and panel feedback; the four SAP cases do not provide this complete workflow to copy.

2.3 BF-03 — Same-warehouse relocation

Trigger: goods must move between locations within one physical warehouse. Warehouse Staff executes without Owner approval or an inter-warehouse Transfer Request (TR).

1. Select the warehouse and source location, then individual balances (separate product/lot/quality/owner rows, searchable by name, SKU or lot) or the whole location, enter a positive whole number of cartons/pieces, one destination location and a reason. Check both locations belong to the same warehouse and differ, the lot is verified, the source quantity is sufficient after other open tasks and no stocktake lock applies. The server simulates the packaging split: sealed cartons stay whole and loose/open portions move whole; cartons are never opened automatically.
2. Create a relocation task (TO-RELOCATE) in OPEN state. Only its creator may edit or cancel it while OPEN. Preserve reservations and demand links for already committed goods.
3. The creator confirms TO-RELOCATE once for the whole order: every line must be submitted and each actual quantity must equal the planned quantity. There is no partial confirmation; to change quantity or destination, edit the OPEN order first or cancel it. Physical movement/counting supports confirmation rather than a standalone software activity.
4. Atomically decrease source/increase destination by the same quantity, preserving warehouse, owner, lot and quality and checking destination capacity. Move the reservation with the stock and retain audit history; warehouse total is unchanged.

End: the task is CONFIRMED in full or CANCELLED before posting; a confirmed relocation is immutable. A physical move to another warehouse requires a separate BF-04 case.

Documents: TO-RELOCATE and location confirmation history. This process uses UC-68, UC-69, UC-70, UC-73, without TR, TD or MWTR.

2.4 BF-04 — Inter-warehouse transfer and discrepancy resolution

Trigger: stock must move between two physical warehouses for balancing or fulfilment. Warehouse requests, Owner approves, and source/destination confirm independently. Same-warehouse relocation uses BF-03 instead.

1. Check compatible availability at configured warehouses (Sales sees only the warehouse catalogue, not operational balances). Direct dispatch from another warehouse uses BF-06 there, without a fictitious receipt at the main warehouse. Warehouse Staff assigned to the source warehouse creates a TR with source and destination (the destination may be outside the creator's assignments), products, units, quantities and reason; it is saved as PENDING_APPROVAL.
2. A different Owner approves, rejects or returns the TR (REVISE → REVISION_REQUIRED). The creator edits and resubmits a returned TR, preserving line IDs. Approval does not reserve stock. Do not add another inter-warehouse order or Owner approval of TO; picking/put-away TOs are internal tasks with execution confirmation.
3. Source Warehouse creates a draft Transfer Dispatch (TD) from approved TR lines, choosing GOOD/STORE balances with verified lots and a source STAGING location. The draft reserves the source quantity at once and cannot exceed the approved remainder; one TR may have several TDs. Cancelling a draft TD releases its reservation, open requirement/task and TR allowance without stock posting.
4. From the TD, Warehouse generates exactly one internal requirement and one PICK TO; the reservation moves to the task. The creator confirms the PICK together with the handover (recipient name, optional vehicle plate): one atomic posting moves source → staging → transit and sets the TD to POSTED. TD is the transfer goods issue; do not post an additional GI for the same quantity.
5. A destination user different from the dispatcher receives against the posted TD, in one or several receipts (GoodsReceipt TRANSFER): GOOD quantities into a RECEIVING location, DAMAGED quantities into QUARANTINE with a damage reason. Each receipt reduces transit by the actual quantity; unreceived quantity stays in transit and receipts cannot exceed it. Preserve demand allocation throughout source → transit → destination.
6. For GOOD receipt lines Warehouse generates one PUTAWAY requirement/TO and confirms receiving → STORAGE without a second receipt increase; an all-DAMAGED receipt needs no TO. Unreceived quantity is reported as a transfer difference record at the destination; a different Owner approves it against a later posted receipt of the same TD, or rejects/returns it, keeping it open. A matched release does not automatically complete the entire TR.
7. Close the TR only when every approved quantity is dispatched, transit is zero, every required GOOD put-away is complete and no difference record is open. When the remaining approved quantity will not be dispatched, a partial close stops further TDs after everything already dispatched has been received and put away. Posted quantities are never edited.

Example: A dispatches 100 boxes; B receives 98: 95 good + 3 broken. B receives 95 good and 3 quarantined; 2 remain transit/unresolved. Do not leave all 100 in transit or treat the 3 physically present damaged boxes as lost. The 2 boxes stay in transit under an open difference record until a later posted receipt is approved; the transfer function does not write them off automatically.

**Inventory effect:** store-managed total including transit is preserved by ordinary transfer; only valid loss/destruction/external issue reduces it.

Documents: TR, TD, handover, MWTR, discrepancy case. SAP WM II distinguishes STO, issue/receipt and put-away TO. [MISA transfer](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-cach-chuyen-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop) distinguishes warehouse moves within a branch and inter-branch transfers. WMSD applies physical warehouses of one store and review1's two-document requirement without inferring multiple branches.

2.5 BF-05 — Stocktake, reconciliation and adjustment

**Trigger:** monthly periodic stocktake per Q14, area counts or suspected discrepancies.

1. Owner plans an ad-hoc session (warehouse, locations, optional lot/product filters, counters) or a recurring stocktake plan (Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly, with reminder days). A plan only reports that a period is due; the Owner generates the PLANNED session for that period. There is no scheduler.
2. Resolve unfinished receipt/issue/transfer tasks, reservations and holds in scope. Separate staged, quarantined, custody and transit stock; never count both ends of a transfer.
3. An assigned counter activates the session: an **immutable cutoff snapshot** is saved and the scope locations are locked in one transaction. The **count sheet** by location/lot/quality is printed from the snapshot and shows the book quantity; there is no blind count (decision of 22 Nov 2026). Newly found items are allowed.
4. Selected MVP: lock quantity/location/status-changing transactions in the counted scope until the session is completed or cancelled. Other areas continue. This replaces the earlier Report 3 post-cutoff reconciliation model. Do not implement partial locking without reconciliation.
5. Counters enter physical carton/piece counts, user and time. Blank means uncounted, not zero; empty locations are confirmed explicitly; found stock needs evidence. Each recount appends a retained count attempt rather than overwriting a previous one.
6. For every line whose newest count differs from the book, the counter records the reason (Damaged, Lost, Misplaced, Found, Record error, Other with note). When every line is counted and every difference has a reason, the counter reconciles the session; nothing is posted and the locks stay.
7. The Owner reviews each line (book, counted, difference, reason) and either requests a recount of selected lines, which returns the session to counting, or approves selected or all lines. The Owner cannot approve a line they counted.
8. Approval posts each nonzero difference once against the snapshot balance (same lot, location, quality and owner) through one stock transaction and one posted stock adjustment; matching lines close without posting. When every line is settled the session is COMPLETED and the locks are released in the same transaction; otherwise it stays reconciling. Failure rolls back the whole approval without unlocking. An unfinished session without settled lines may be cancelled with a reason, which releases the locks.

Feasibility: D5 selected locking; the following advanced alternative is not selected. Count small areas with Owner available to avoid prolonged locks. If operations must continue during counting, expected quantity at count time = snapshot + receipts after cutoff − issues after cutoff; delta = physical count − expected quantity. Apply delta to current stock, never replace current stock with an old count. This requires per-line count timestamps and late/backdated movement controls. These are distinct designs and cannot be mixed casually.

Documents: session, count sheet, counts/recounts, variance record, adjustment request, approval and increase/decrease documents. SAP WM IV pp. 2, 4–6, 12–17, 20–21, 26–27 supports freeze/recount/clearing concepts, not a requirement to implement SAP WM/MM dual books or value accounting. [MISA stocktake](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-cach-kiem-ke-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop) informs quantity/reason/document handling; immutable snapshot and approval controls are WMSD To-be.

2.6 BF-06 - Sales order and direct warehouse issue

Trigger: Sales receives an externally verified order. Buyer/contact details are recorded for that order without a customer catalogue screen or prerequisite customer-master creation.

1. Sales records the source order reference/evidence and requested product quantities, units, conversion and permitted price snapshots. Reject duplicate source orders and preserve original line identities.
2. Warehouse creates a DRAFT goods issue directly from the saved SO. Select the assigned warehouse and actual eligible GOOD/STORE stock, lots and locations. Several issues may serve one order; active drafts and posted issues share the remaining order allowance.
3. At actual warehouse handover, Warehouse rechecks source quantity, current versions, available stock, packaging, holds and stocktake locks, then posts the issue once. Record the recipient and time. No WIR, OD, sales picking task, staging move or delivery trip is generated.
4. Sales records externally evidenced customer receipt against the posted issue under UC-105. Keep recipient, time, quantities and private evidence on the issue. This supports customer-return eligibility and order progress; it is not a delivery-planning or trip-tracking workflow.
5. If unreceived goods physically return, Warehouse records their actual quantity, quality, destination, reason and evidence on that issue. Post one linked receipt and restore only actual returned stock. No separate failed-delivery request or inspection worklist is required. Reopen only the corresponding source-order allowance.
6. Sales may edit/cancel only eligible unexecuted remainder. Customer returns for goods already received use BF-07 and never automatically reopen sales demand. External payment confirmation remains UC-176, with no money movement in WMSD.

2.7 BF-07 - Customer returns

Customer returns remain in scope. Source them from evidenced customer-received goods on a posted issue or Accountant-verified external history. Buyer details come from the source document; no customer catalogue lookup is required. Unreceived goods use the actual intake action on the original issue in BF-06.

1. Sales creates the CR with source lines, quantity, reason and evidence. Per source, posted returns plus open claims cannot exceed verified customer-received quantity. Unknown sources wait for verification under UC-173.
2. Warehouse inspects actual quantity, lot, packaging, use/installation, damage cause and deadline evidence. Inspection records facts only; it creates no custody balance or owned stock.
3. Sales accepts/rejects ordinary eligible quantities after inspection and may be the CR creator. Owner decides only eligible, substantiated exceptions with a different proposer. Installed, customer-damaged or verified-overdue goods remain excluded; unknown eligibility stays pending.
4. Warehouse posts only the current accepted quantity as CUSTOMER_RETURN receipt. GOOD goes to the permitted receiving/storage flow and accepted defects to quarantine. Acceptance itself changes no stock; receipt posts STORE stock once.
5. Record rejected handback with actual recipient, quantity and evidence, without a stock ledger entry. Close warehouse work after quantities are resolved. Preserve the original order/issue and receipt evidence; no automatic refund or reopened sales demand.

2.8 BF-08 — Supplier return and quantity reconciliation

**Trigger:** wrong/defective goods or other supplier-agreed returns from BF-01/02/07 or existing stock.

1. Warehouse proposes return linked to supplier, source PO/receipt, lot/location, quantity, reason and evidence. Defects are immediately blocked; good goods proposed for return are reserved for the case.
2. Accountant verifies documents and records supplier agreement/refusal, quantity, schedule, handover method and references. Q20 also permits Owner/purchasing personnel to contact suppliers externally; Accountant still records WMS evidence without a purchasing role. Supplier has no WMSD login.
3. Accountant verifies supplier agreement against source/lines/quantity before dispatch; Warehouse checks case quantity and duplicate handling. BF-08 requires no Owner dispatch, variance or closure approval. A historical BF-02 decision is a reference if present, never a mandatory dispatch prerequisite.
4. Warehouse creates draft GI-SR against source and supplier-agreed quantity, records correct-lot picking and confirms actual handover. Missing agreement or unclear source/quantity keeps the case open without dispatch; supplier refusal does not make defective goods available.
5. Record actual handover/issue. Store-owned stock deducts once; goods rejected at arrival and held for supplier custody only reduce custody.
6. Accountant records supplier receipt evidence; Warehouse provides transport evidence. Compare dispatched versus acknowledged quantity. Short/damaged/partially refused amounts remain open; do not rewrite dispatch to the supplier's desired quantity.
7. Accountant reconciles and records supplier-agreed resolution per quantity; Warehouse confirms supporting physical documents. Unresolved disputes remain open without a BF-08 Owner approval step. Goods coming back increase stock only after linked receipt/inspection; compensation does not recreate quantities. Destruction/release of defective stock still on site uses BF-02 authorization.
8. Accountant records Invoice/Credit Note or applicable external references through UC-174: type, ID/date, purchase/GI-SR lines and reconciled quantity, evidence/verification state. ,  messages do not prove debt adjustment; unverified finance remains PENDING_EXTERNAL and no Credit Note is required before dispatch. Accountant closes warehouse work through UC-128 when every quantity reconciles to Warehouse-confirmed documents, without Owner approval; finance remains independent and closure makes no further deduction.

BF-08 inventory effect: SR/agreement verification does not reduce total; actual Warehouse dispatch reduces source-correct owned/custody stock once. ACK/closure never deducts again. Replacement requires actual new receipt, never stock from agreement/money.

BF-08 documents: SR, Accountant-verified supplier agreement, GI-SR or CUS-OUT/HO, ACK/VAR, reconciliation outcome and EXT-REF. No Owner approval. Section 13 MISA purchased-goods-return guidance is reference only, not a WMSD approval/accounting requirement.

Each user, including inactive accounts, must have exactly one role: one of the five default roles OWNER/SALES/WAREHOUSE/ACCOUNTANT/ADMIN or an Administrator-created custom role; many users may share a role. Permissions come only from the current role, with no combined roles or session role switching. Assigned warehouses remain independent and may include multiple warehouses. Role changes affect subsequent requests and never rewrite historical actors or decisions. Warehouse Staff is the canonical name for the existing Inventory Staff role, not an additional role. Each department records and confirms its own work using personal accounts (D2). Purchasing and delivery personnel may be external business participants but receive no new application role (D3). Survey-reported delegation does not automatically confer software approval rights.

	Approve replenishment, TR, transfer differences, OTHER receipts, scoped on-site dispositions, eligible CR exceptions and B1 resolution; plan stocktake and perform Clear Difference; maintain products, UOM/conversions and the warehouse/location catalogue. 	2

	Agree terms with the supplier, then create, edit (DRAFT only), issue and reschedule PO after RR approval; close the unreceived PO remainder; decide supplier custody; verify supplier-return agreements, reconcile ACK/variances and close supplier-return warehouse work via UC-128, ; record independent external Invoice/Credit Note references. 	3

	Receive, inspect, put away, post direct goods issues and actual receipts, relocate, count and isolate within assigned warehouses. Transfer confirmations remain independent; Warehouse does not confirm customer receipt on behalf of Sales.
	4
	Sales Staff
	Copy verified external SO with document-level buyer information; record issue-linked customer receipt evidence and external payment; accept/reject ordinary customer returns after inspection. No customer catalogue or delivery planning.
	5
	Administrator
	Manage staff accounts, lock or unlock them, and request email OTPs for password recovery (UC-175). Maintain custom roles from the fixed permission catalogue, role and warehouse assignments, and technical configuration within administrative scope.

5.3 Background Job Inventory

Optional SMTP delivery uses its own success/failure/attempt tracking; uncertain delivery requires reconciliation.

II. Use Case Specifications
All active operations require authenticated active user, exact server-side operation/sub-operation and warehouse/record/evidence scope. View does not grant mutation. Source/version is rechecked; typed decisions bind document/version/line/qty and proper actual users; C2 allows Sales own ordinary CR, while Owner approvals require distinct requester. Stock effects commit source/balance/ledger/reservation/status/audit/outbox atomically with stable operation/idempotency keys. Failed confirmation leaves no partial change. Freeze blocks mutation except explicitly authorized clearing.

1. Replenishment, Purchasing and Receipt Tracking (FE-01)
The following operations require an active user with the applicable permissions and record scope. Section III defines the screen fields.

UC-01: View replenishment request list
Sales and Warehouse Staff view their own requests within assigned warehouses. Accountant views requests in assigned warehouses and Store Owner views requests across warehouses. Use SC-06.1 to search, filter and open a request.

UC-02: View replenishment request details
An authorized reader opens one request to see its lines, demand sources, quantities, review history and visible purchase orders. Use SC-06.3.

UC-07: View purchase order list
Store Owner, Accountant and Warehouse Staff view the orders allowed for their role and warehouse scope. Use SC-07.1 to search, filter and open an order. Purchase prices are limited to the financial roles.

UC-08: View purchase order details
An authorized reader opens an order to see item progress, linked documents and each delivery's inspection and receipt history. Use SC-07.2.

UC-15: View goods receipt list
An authorized reader filters scoped receipt records by type, status, warehouse or creation month and opens an eligible result. Use SC-08.3.

UC-16: View goods receipt details
An authorized reader opens a receipt to view its status, item quantities, lots, locations and available source links. For a purchase receipt, use SC-08.4. The original inspection shows counted quantities and custody or rejection decisions.

3. Goods Inspection and Goods Receipt (FE-03)
This feature covers purchase receiving: goods inspection and the purchase goods receipt (GR). Other goods receipts and custody receipts are specified separately. Warehouse Staff is the only role that creates, confirms and posts these documents; Store Owner and Accountant may assist physically but hold no posting rights. Stale versions are refused with MSG-05, a repeated command with the same key returns the committed result (MSG-06), and a locked stocktake location blocks posting (MSG-09).

3.1 UC-37: Create Goods Inspection
| Primary Actors | Warehouse Staff | Secondary Actors | Store Owner, Accountant (may assist physically) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Description | As Warehouse Staff, I want to record each delivery against its purchase order, with the actual counts, lots and quality, so that only the accepted quantity becomes store-owned stock. The inspection is saved as a draft and confirmed within this use case. Confirming creates the receipt documents but changes no stock. | | |
| Preconditions | A purchase order is OPEN or PARTIAL for a warehouse assigned to the actor. The delivered lots exist and are verified. At least one active RECEIVING location exists in the warehouse. | | |
| Normal Flow | 1. The actor starts the inspection from the purchase order or from the inspection list and selects the order. The system shows the supplier, warehouse and the lines with their outstanding quantities. | | |
| | 2. The actor enters the arrival time (not in the future), the delivery note number and the deliverer. | | |
| | 3. For each delivered lot the actor adds a line: the order line, the verified lot, the counted quantity and its unit (carton or base unit), the quality (Good, Damaged, Pending, Blocked), the packaging (Sealed, Loose, Opened, Torn, Unknown), the ownership decision (Accept, Hold in custody, Reject), the RECEIVING location for accepted goods, and a note. An order line delivered in several lots is split into several lines. | | |
| | 4. The actor selects Save draft. The system saves the inspection as DRAFT together with the arrival record. | | |
| | 5. The actor selects Confirm. The system checks that, for each order line, the accepted quantity in base units does not exceed the outstanding quantity less the quantity already in draft receipts, and that each GOOD / ACCEPT line has an eligible RECEIVING destination. The system requires CUSTODY for every line with another quality. | | |
| | 6. The system sets the inspection to CONFIRMED and the arrival to RECORDED. It creates a draft purchase GR for the accepted lines and a draft custody receipt, owned by the supplier, for the lines held in custody. Rejected lines create no document. The actor is taken to the new document. | | |
| Alternative Flows | A1 (step 1, partial delivery): each delivery is a separate inspection; the order stays open until it is received in full or its remainder is closed (UC-12). | | |
| | A2 (step 3, excess, wrong lot or doubtful goods): record the actual verified lot of the ordered product, including a lot different from the one on the order. Good goods may be accepted within the PO allowance. Record excess as custody or rejection as applicable, and record doubtful goods as custody. Capture any difference in the line decision and note. | | |
| | A3 (step 3, packaging): torn packaging alone is not damage and the quality stays Good. For opened or loose goods the actor counts actual pieces. Sealed lines must be whole cartons of the lot's pieces per carton. | | |
| | A4 (step 3, quality): Damaged, Pending or Blocked goods must use custody. Post the custody receipt under UC-50. Start a supplier return from a posted custody line under BF-08. UC-51 to UC-54 describe the backend custody resolution operations. | | |
| | A5 (step 6, nothing accepted): no purchase receipt is created. Held quantities generate a draft custody receipt. If all quantities are rejected, only the arrival and confirmed inspection are retained. The order’s outstanding quantity is unchanged. | | |
| | E1 (validation): arrival time in the future or earlier than the lot's production date; an unverified lot (MSG-10); a quantity that does not convert exactly to the base unit; a location that is not an active RECEIVING location of the warehouse (MSG-02). | | |
| | E2: the order is no longer open, or accepted quantity exceeds the allowance (MSG-03). | | |
| Postconditions | The inspection is DRAFT or CONFIRMED. A confirmed inspection creates a draft purchase GR when there are accepted lines and a draft custody receipt when there are held lines. Stock and the order's received quantity are unchanged. | | |

3.15 UC-51: Reinspect Custody Goods

3.16 UC-52: Record Custody Resolution Decision
Accountant records Accept or Return for scoped lines and quantities on a POSTED custody receipt, with a reason and evidence. Accept requires a GOOD inspection result for the selected goods. The decision records authority for the selected quantities. Stock is unchanged.

3.17 UC-53: Convert Custody Goods to Purchase Receipt
Warehouse Staff selects quantities approved for acceptance, an eligible receiving location and an open source PO with remaining allowance. The system creates a draft purchase receipt linked to the held stock and the decision. On posting under UC-40, the system transfers the relevant quantities from supplier ownership to store ownership and updates received quantities on the PO.

3.18 UC-54: Post Custody Release
After the return agreement, Warehouse Staff records the actual handover and posts the custody release against the approved quantity. The system deducts supplier-owned custody stock, preserves the order's received quantity, and links the release to the source lines. For a supplier-return request, see UC-124.

III. Functional Requirements
The screen layouts and field contracts below support responsive phone use for actual carton/piece/m² quantities, location references and photos. Native mobile applications, offline use and automatic barcode capture are outside scope.
Shared field contract: external provider version is optional if unavailable; internal document/verification version and stable nonblank correlation are retained without inventing a provider revision. ObjectId references, Decimal128 quantities/factors, whole actual pieces for whole-base-unit products, immutable conversion snapshots, UTC Date events displayed in Vietnam time. Required fields are those at the linked UC stage. No invented quantity/factor/deadline/warehouse defaults. Filters optional/nonpersistent; derived balances/approval actor/time/before-after read-only. Positive execution quantity, zero count allowed, blank uncounted, signed nonzero adjustment. Finite states use String enums; version/attempt uses positive Int32, active/found flags Boolean, identifiers/text are trimmed nonblank String where required, evidence uses controlled object-key/hash references.

IV. Non-Functional Requirements
1. External Interfaces
Responsive internal web application including phone. MongoDB runtime requires supported atomic multi-document transactions. SQL Server/T-SQL is reference DDL only. Server-side SMTP API-01 and controlled evidence API-02; no MISA/payment/tax integration. Warehouse outputs derive from committed snapshots and scoped evidence access.
2. Quality Attributes
2.1 Usability
Actual boxes/loose pieces, visible conversion, warehouse/location/lot identity, phone camera/file attachments and per-quantity return/quality review. Missing customer-receipt evidence keeps that issue action pending; retry updates evidence without repeating PGI. Show copied SO, PGI, DELIVERED and COMPLETED as distinct milestones. Show physical/available/reserved/custody/transit separately. Distinguish draft, review, physical post, print and external finance status. Errors identify correction without losing valid input or exposing protected data. RR forms show per-line warnings (open RR, incoming PO, sufficient stock) with related documents, require an explanation for warned lines, retain quantity/date/reasons when evaluation changes, and show source links per line. In-app notifications show read/unread per recipient; reading is distinct from resolving a shortage.
2.2 Performance
No signed-off numeric response SLA, concurrency count, volume or availability percentage supplied. Verify scoped list/report behavior on representative store-approved datasets; agree numeric targets before acceptance.
2.3 Security
Active personal accounts, exactly one role per user (five default roles or Administrator-created custom roles built from the fixed permission catalogue) and server-side operation/warehouse/record/evidence scope, including direct requests/exports. Distinct requester for Owner approvals and independent transfer dispatcher/receiver; C2 Sales own ordinary CR acceptance is explicitly allowed, BF-08 Owner approval is absent. Administrator no default business rights. Protect credentials/server secrets, private evidence retrieval and administrative audit; changed access never rewrites past decisions.
For UC-152 and UC-175, WMSD binds each OTP to the target account, expires it and allows it to be consumed once. Passwords and OTPs are excluded from application logs.
2.4 Reliability and Data Integrity
Integrity controls include shared source guards across issue-linked customer receipt evidence, actual unreceived-goods intake and CR claims; actual intake posts stock and restores only source-order allowance atomically; B1 balance-neutral holds, current approved plan plus Warehouse execution, delta/RECLASS batch and no early unlock. No Owner supplier-return approval route is in scope.
Atomically commit source/version, batch, movements, balance, reservations, status, audit and outbox. Stable operation/idempotency key and hash prevent duplicate stock effects; changed payload conflicts. Unknown commit resolved by lookup; transient retry uses same identity. Concurrent holds/receipts/returns respect eligible source ceilings. All in-scope quantity/quality/ownership/location changes and backdated corrections coordinate with stocktake activation/freeze; only authorized clearing changes counted balance before unlock. Interrupted session retains freeze/history. Snapshot, count attempts, posted documents/assessments remain immutable; corrections append links. New documents receive _id = documentNo ([type]-[warehouse/department]-[YYMMDD]-[6-digit sequence]) inside the same transaction, retrying on collision; IDs never change after save.
Issue receipt evidence and PAY-CFM use stable business-operation IDs/hash, without stock movements. Atomically commit confirmation/VOIDED correction with SO version/progress, audit/outbox. Competing confirmations contend on shared issue/SO guards, not merely snapshot sums. PAY-CFM binds current delivered SO version; correction invalidates stale completion conditions.
RR saves re-evaluate advisory warnings inside the transaction and use stable command identities; the shared coverage guard was removed on 11/11/2026 and duplicate purchasing is controlled by confirmed explanations and independent Owner review. Form opening/cancellation has no persistence effect. Post-commit Notification retries preserve existing readAt and deduplicate event/type/recipient/warehouse without creating RR. Output/notification retry never repeats stock posting. SMTP uncertain timeout requires delivery reconciliation, not a claim of exactly-once email. Transit/received/resolved reconcile; custody never silently becomes owned stock.
2.5 Maintainability and Traceability
174 active UCs and 19 retired identifiers map to feature, permission, screen, inputs, alternate flows and exact inventory effect. Eight retired screens retain historical identifiers. Trace source document/line/version -> SO -> direct GI -> issue receipt evidence -> PAY-CFM and PO -> receipt/transfer -> return/disposition/adjustment, with actor/time/evidence. Reconcile closing = opening + ledger delta; preserve immutable stock history and source claims.
